Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know ahead of your session.

Where do the lessons take place?
Sessions are hosted directly at Kabalana Beach. The precise meeting point coordinates and local details are confirmed via WhatsApp before you arrive.
What should I bring along?
Just your swimwear, a towel, and sun protection. We supply all surfboards, leashes, and rashguards.
Are complete beginners welcome?
Absolutely. Our beginner programs are explicitly designed to support people who have never stepped foot on a surfboard before.

Ready to surf Kabalana?

Connect with our local team on WhatsApp to coordinate your schedule and secure your board.
